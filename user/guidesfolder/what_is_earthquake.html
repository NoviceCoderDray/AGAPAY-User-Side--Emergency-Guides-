An earthquake is the shaking of the ground caused by a sudden movement of the Earth's crust. This movement happens along faults where stress builds up until it is released.

Ang lindol ay ang pagyanig ng lupa na dulot ng biglaang paggalaw ng crust ng mundo. Nangyayari ito sa mga fault kapag naiipon ang tensyon at biglang napakawalan.

Magnitude vs Intensity

- Magnitude is the amount of energy released at the source of the earthquake. It is measured using the Richter scale or Moment Magnitude (Mw).

- Ang Magnitude ay tumutukoy sa enerhiyang nailalabas ng lindol mula sa pinagmulan nito.

- Intensity describes how strong the shaking is at a specific location. In the Philippines, the PHIVOLCS Earthquake Intensity Scale (PEIS) is used.

- Ang Intensity ay nagpapakita kung gaano kalakas ang pagyanig sa isang partikular na lugar. Sa Pilipinas, ginagamit ang PHIVOLCS Earthquake Intensity Scale (PEIS).

Magnitude Examples

  • Magnitude 1.0 – 1.9: Micro — usually not felt, recorded only by seismographs.
  • Magnitude 2.0 – 2.9: Very minor — rarely noticed; might be felt very slightly by a few people close to epicenter.
  • Magnitude 3.0 – 3.9: Weak — felt by people indoors; like a light vibration; no damage expected.
  • Magnitude 4.0 – 4.9: Light — noticeable shaking; rattles windows and dishes; minor non-structural damage possible.
  • Magnitude 5.0 – 5.9: Moderate — felt by most people in the area; can cause minor to moderate damage to poorly built structures.
  • Magnitude 6.0 – 6.9: Strong — can cause serious damage in populated areas; structures built to code may sustain damage.
  • Magnitude 7.0 – 7.9: Major — widespread, heavy damage; many buildings and infrastructure affected over large areas.
  • Magnitude 8.0 – 8.9: Great — severe to catastrophic damage across very large regions; long-lasting recovery.
  • Magnitude 9.0+: Very great — extraordinary, devastating destruction over vast areas; extremely rare but catastrophic.

PHIVOLCS Intensity Scale Examples

  • Intensity I: Scarcely perceptible
  • Intensity II: Slightly felt
  • Intensity III: Weak shaking, felt indoors
  • Intensity IV: Light, may cause hanging objects to swing
  • Intensity V: Moderate, may disturb light sleepers
  • Intensity VI: Strong, may cause small cracks
  • Intensity VII: Very strong, difficulty standing
  • Intensity VIII+: Destructive to very destructive

Halimbawa: Kapag ang lindol ay may Magnitude 6.5, pero Intensity IV lang sa inyong lugar, ito ay malakas ngunit hindi malubhang pagyanig.

Can Earthquakes Be Predicted?

No, earthquakes cannot be predicted. Scientists can identify areas at risk, but the exact time and location cannot be determined. That’s why being prepared is important.

Hindi maaaring hulaan ang eksaktong oras at lugar ng lindol. Kaya mahalagang maging handa palagi.
