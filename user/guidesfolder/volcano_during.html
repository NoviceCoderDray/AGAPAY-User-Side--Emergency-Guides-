During a Volcanic Eruption
Safety Tips

This video explains what to do during a volcanic eruption.

Clip from: "What to do before, during and after volcanic eruptions" by Yarah Jallorina on YouTube

1. Stay indoors

Remain inside to avoid ashfall and dangerous debris.

Manatili sa loob ng bahay upang maiwasan ang abo at mga mapanganib na debris.

2. Close all windows and doors

Seal openings to prevent volcanic ash from entering.

Isara ang lahat ng bintana at pinto upang hindi makapasok ang abo.

3. Wear masks and goggles

Protect your lungs and eyes when going outside if necessary.

Magsuot ng mask at goggles para maprotektahan ang baga at mata kung lalabas.

4. Follow evacuation orders

Evacuate immediately when instructed by authorities.

Sumunod agad sa utos ng paglilikas mula sa mga awtoridad.

5. Avoid rivers and low-lying areas

Stay away from riverbanks as lahar and mudflows may occur.

Iwasan ang mga ilog at mabababang lugar dahil maaaring magkaroon ng lahar at mudflow.

6. Stay informed

Listen to PHIVOLCS and local officials for real-time updates.

Makinig sa PHIVOLCS at lokal na pamahalaan para sa mga update.

7. Avoid driving

Ashfall reduces visibility and can damage engines.

Ang abo ay nakababawas ng tanawin at nakakasira ng makina.
