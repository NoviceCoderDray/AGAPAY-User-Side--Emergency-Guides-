First Aid for Burns
Steps to Follow Immediately

This video demonstrates quick first aid steps for minor burns.

A video from: "First Aid: How to Treat Burns" by RafflesHospital on YouTube

1. Cool the burn immediately

Hold the burned area under cool (not cold) running water for at least 10 minutes to stop the burning process.

Ilubog ang nasunog na bahagi sa malamig (hindi malamig na yelo) na umaagos na tubig nang hindi bababa sa 10 minuto upang mapahinto ang pagkalat ng init.

2. Remove tight items

Carefully remove rings, bracelets, or clothing near the burn before the area swells.

Maingat na alisin ang singsing, pulseras, o damit malapit sa paso bago ito mamaga.

3. Cover the burn

Use a clean, non-stick bandage or cloth to protect the area and reduce infection risk.

Gamitin ang malinis at hindi dumidikit na benda o tela upang takpan ang paso at mabawasan ang panganib ng impeksyon.

4. Do NOT apply ice or creams

Avoid using ice, toothpaste, or butter on burns as they can worsen the injury.

Iwasang lagyan ng yelo, toothpaste, o mantikilya ang paso dahil maaari nitong palalain ang pinsala.

5. Seek medical help if needed

If the burn is deep, large, or on the face or joints, go to a health facility immediately.

Kung malalim, malaki, o nasa mukha o kasu-kasuan ang paso, agad pumunta sa pinakamalapit na pasilidad pangkalusugan.
