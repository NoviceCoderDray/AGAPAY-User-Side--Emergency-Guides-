Responding to a
Vehicle Accident

This video explains how to respond safely during a vehicle accident.

Clip from: "WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU GET INTO A CAR ACCIDENT IN PHILIPPINES (TAGALOG)" by REAL RYAN on YouTube

1. Avoid arguments

Stay calm and avoid confronting other parties involved in the accident.

Manatiling kalmado at iwasan ang pakikipagtalo sa ibang sangkot sa aksidente.

2. Exchange information

Share names, contact numbers, license plate numbers, and insurance details.

Magpalitan ng pangalan, numero, plaka, at insurance details.

3. Report to the police

File a police report, especially if there are injuries or major property damage.

Mag-ulat sa pulis lalo na kung may sugatan o malaking pinsala.

4. Contact your insurance provider

Notify your insurance company immediately to start the claims process.

Agad na ipaalam sa iyong insurance provider upang masimulan ang claim process.

5. Call emergency services

Dial local emergency hotlines for medical help and police assistance.

Tumawag agad sa mga emergency hotline para sa tulong medikal at pulisya.

6. Provide first aid if safe

Give basic first aid to injured persons while waiting for help.

Magbigay ng pangunahing lunas kung ligtas habang naghihintay ng tulong.

7. Document the accident

Take photos, videos, and note details for insurance and authorities.

Kumuha ng mga larawan, video, at tala para sa insurance at awtoridad.
