During a Flood
Safety Tips

This video explains what to do to stay safe during a flood.

Clip from: "FLASH FLOODS: What to do before, during and after" by Creative Media Unit on YouTube

1. Evacuate when advised

Follow instructions from authorities and move to higher ground immediately.

Sumunod sa mga awtoridad at lumikas agad papunta sa mataas na lugar.

2. Avoid floodwater

Do not walk or drive through moving water; just six inches can knock you down.

Iwasan ang paglalakad o pagmamaneho sa baha; anim na pulgada lang ng tubig ay maaaring magpatumba.

3. Stay away from electricity

Avoid downed power lines and unplug appliances to prevent electrocution.

Iwasan ang mga nakabuwal na kable at tanggalin sa saksakan ang mga gamit para maiwasan ang kuryente.

4. Stay in safe shelters

Stay in designated evacuation centers until authorities declare it safe.

Manatili sa evacuation center hanggang sa payagan ng mga awtoridad na ligtas nang bumalik.

5. Stay informed

Listen to official announcements for real-time updates and evacuation instructions. Always follow the advice of local authorities.

Makinig sa ssopisyal na anunsyo para sa mga pinakabagong balita at tagubilin sa paglikas. Laging sundin ang payo ng mga awtoridad.

6. Keep yourself dry

Avoid prolonged exposure to floodwater to prevent infections.

Iwasang mababad sa baha upang makaiwas sa sakit at impeksyon.

7. Assist others if possible

Help children, elderly, and people with disabilities during evacuation.

Tulungan ang mga bata, matatanda, at may kapansanan sa panahon ng paglikas.
