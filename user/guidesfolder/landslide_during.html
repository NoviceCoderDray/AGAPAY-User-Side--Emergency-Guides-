During a Landslide
Tips to Stay Safe

This video demonstrates what to do during a landslide.

Clip from: "LANDSLIDE-DISASTER PREPAREDNESS-BEFORE,DURING AND AFTER" by Daniel GARDETV on YouTube

1. Stay calm and alert

Keep calm and think clearly. Being alert helps you make safer choices when the ground starts moving.

Manatiling kalmado at maging alerto. Makakatulong ito para makapagdesisyon ng ligtas habang nagaganap ang pagguho.

2. Move to higher ground immediately

If you are in the path of a landslide, go to higher and safer ground as fast as possible.

Agad na lumipat sa mataas at ligtas na lugar kung nasa landas ng pagguho.

3. Stay away from rivers and streams

Landslides can block waterways and cause sudden flooding.

Lumayo sa mga ilog at sapa dahil maaaring magdulot ng biglaang pagbaha ang pagguho.

4. If indoors, move to a safe area

Stay away from windows and doors. Move to the most stable part of the building.

Kung nasa loob ng bahay, lumipat sa ligtas na bahagi at lumayo sa mga bintana at pinto.

5. Listen to authorities

Follow evacuation orders and updates from local officials or disaster agencies.

Makinig sa mga abiso ng lokal na pamahalaan at sundin ang mga kautusan sa paglikas.

6. Avoid roads in landslide-prone areas

Do not drive or walk along roads that may collapse or be blocked by debris.

Iwasang dumaan sa mga kalsadang maaaring gumuho o barado ng mga debris.
