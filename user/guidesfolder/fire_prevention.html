Fire Prevention
Tips you should Follow

This video shows common ways to prevent fire incidents.

A video from: "Top 10 Fire Safety Tips" by LIFESAVER

1. Unplug appliances when not in use

Turn off and unplug appliances like irons, rice cookers, and fans when you're not using them to avoid overheating or short circuits.

I-off at tanggalin sa saksakan ang mga gamit tulad ng plantsa, rice cooker, at electric fan kapag hindi ginagamit upang maiwasan ang sobrang init o short circuit.

2. Always check gas stoves and tanks

Make sure gas stoves are turned off and check for leaks regularly. If you smell gas, open windows and do not use electrical switches.

Siguraduhing nakapatay ang gas stove at regular na tingnan kung may tagas. Kapag may naamoy na gas, buksan ang mga bintana at huwag gumamit ng mga switch.

3. Don’t overload electrical outlets

Plugging too many devices in one outlet can overheat wires and start a fire. Use a power strip with a breaker if needed.

Ang pagsaksak ng maraming gamit sa isang outlet ay maaaring magdulot ng sobrang init at sunog. Gumamit ng power strip na may breaker kung kinakailangan.

4. Keep matches and lighters away from children

Store them in high places out of reach of kids to prevent accidents or fire play.

Itago ang mga posporo at lighter sa mataas na lugar na hindi maaabot ng mga bata upang maiwasan ang aksidente o paglalaro ng apoy.

5. Have a fire extinguisher ready

Keep one in the kitchen or near potential fire sources. Make sure everyone at home knows how to use it.

Maglagay ng fire extinguisher sa kusina o malapit sa mga posibleng pag-umpisahan ng sunog. Siguraduhing alam ng lahat sa bahay kung paano ito gamitin.

6. Be careful with candles

Never leave candles burning unattended. Place them on a stable surface away from anything flammable.

Huwag iwanang nakasindi ang kandila nang walang nagbabantay. Ilagay ito sa patag at matibay na lugar na malayo sa anumang madaling magliyab.

7. Install smoke alarms

Smoke alarms alert you early if a fire starts. Test them monthly and replace the batteries when needed.

Nagbibigay babala ang smoke alarm kung may apoy na nagsisimula. Subukan ito buwan-buwan at palitan ang baterya kung kinakailangan.
