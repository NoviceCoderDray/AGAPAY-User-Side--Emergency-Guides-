Signs That May
Indicate a Typhoon

1. Dark, Thick Clouds

The sky becomes unusually dark with thick, fast-moving clouds before a typhoon.

Nagiging sobrang madilim ang kalangitan na may makakapal at mabilis na ulap bago ang isang bagyo.

2. Sudden Strong Winds

Strong gusts of wind may arrive even before the rain starts.

Maaaring maramdaman ang biglaang malalakas na hangin kahit bago magsimula ang ulan.

3. Continuous Heavy Rain

Prolonged heavy rainfall usually signals that a typhoon is approaching.

Ang matagal na malakas na ulan ay karaniwang hudyat ng papalapit na bagyo.

4. Unusual Rise of Sea Water

Storm surges and unusually high waves are signs of an incoming typhoon near coastal areas.

Ang biglaang pagtaas ng tubig-dagat at malalaking alon ay palatandaan ng papalapit na bagyo.

5. Weather Bureau Warnings

Official typhoon warnings from PAGASA or local authorities should always be taken seriously.

Ang mga opisyal na babala mula sa PAGASA o lokal na awtoridad ay dapat laging seryosohin.
