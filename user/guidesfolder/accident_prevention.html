Vehicle Accident Prevention
Safety Tips

This video provides tips on how to prevent road accidents.

A video from: "Top 10 Tips to Prevent Car Accidents | Road Safety Tips" by CAR GAUGE on YouTube

1. Always wear your seatbelt

Buckle up at all times to reduce injury risk in case of an accident.

Laging magsuot ng seatbelt upang mabawasan ang panganib ng pinsala sa aksidente.

2. Avoid distractions while driving

Stay focused—do not text, call, or use gadgets while driving.

Magpokus sa pagmamaneho—iwasang gumamit ng cellphone o gadgets habang nagmamaneho.

3. Follow speed limits

Obey traffic signs and maintain safe driving speeds.

Sumunod sa speed limit at mga traffic signs para sa kaligtasan.

4. Maintain your vehicle

Regularly check brakes, tires, lights, and engine to avoid malfunctions.

Regular na suriin ang preno, gulong, ilaw, at makina upang maiwasan ang aberya.

5. Never drink and drive

Alcohol impairs judgment and reaction time—always assign a sober driver.

Nakakaapekto ang alak sa tamang pag-iisip at reflexes—huwag magmaneho nang lasing.

6. Keep a safe distance

Maintain at least a 3-second gap from the vehicle in front.

Magbigay ng sapat na distansya (hindi bababa sa 3 segundo) mula sa sasakyan sa unahan.

7. Rest before long drives

Avoid drowsy driving by sleeping well before long trips.

Iwasang magmaneho nang inaantok—magpahinga muna bago bumiyahe nang malayo.
