After a Typhoon
Tips for Safety

This video explains safety steps to take after a typhoon.

Clip from: "WHAT TO DO BEFORE, DURING AND AFTER TYPHOON?" by Thea010 on YouTube

1. Stay informed

Listen to official announcements before leaving your home.

Makinig muna sa mga opisyal na abiso bago lumabas ng bahay.

2. Watch out for hazards

Be cautious of fallen power lines, debris, and damaged roads.

Mag-ingat sa mga bumagsak na kuryente, debris, at nasirang daan.

3. Drink safe water

Boil water before drinking to prevent waterborne diseases.

Pakuluan muna ang tubig bago inumin upang maiwasan ang sakit.

4. Inspect your home

Check for structural damage before staying inside again.

Suriin muna kung may pinsala ang bahay bago tumuloy muli sa loob.

5. Help others in need

Assist neighbors, especially the elderly, children, and people with disabilities.

Tumulong sa kapitbahay lalo na sa matatanda, bata, at PWD.

6. Stay safe in evacuation centers

Follow the rules of the evacuation center. Keep your belongings secure, practice good hygiene, and cooperate with authorities.

Sundin ang mga patakaran ng evacuation center. Ingatan ang iyong mga gamit, panatilihin ang kalinisan, at makipagtulungan sa mga awtoridad.

7. Report damages

Inform local authorities about damaged infrastructure and dangerous areas.

Iulat sa lokal na pamahalaan ang mga nasirang imprastruktura at mapanganib na lugar.
