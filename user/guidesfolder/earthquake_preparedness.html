Before an Earthquake
Prepare for Safety

This video explains how to prepare before an earthquake occurs.

A clip from: "What To Do Before, During and After an Earthquake (Science 6)" by Teacher Apole on YouTube

1. Prepare an emergency kit

Include water, food, flashlight, radio, batteries, and first aid supplies.

Maghanda ng emergency kit na may tubig, pagkain, flashlight, radyo, baterya, at first aid.

2. Secure heavy items

Bolt down shelves, TVs, and appliances to avoid tipping or falling.

Siguraduhing nakakabit nang maayos ang mga mabibigat na gamit gaya ng estante at TV.

3. Identify safe spots indoors

Know where to Drop, Cover, and Hold — under sturdy tables or inside corners.

Alamin kung saan ligtas yumuko at magtago, tulad ng ilalim ng matibay na mesa o sulok ng silid.

4. Make a family emergency plan

Discuss where to meet and how to contact each other after a quake.

Magkaroon ng plano ang pamilya kung saan magkikita at paano mag-uusap pagkatapos ng lindol.

5. Join earthquake drills

Regular practice helps everyone know what to do during a quake.

Sumali sa earthquake drill para alam ng lahat ang tamang gagawin sa oras ng lindol.

6. Save emergency contact numbers

Post or store important phone numbers in your phone and home.

Itago ang mga importanteng numero ng emergency sa telepono o isulat sa isang lugar sa bahay.
