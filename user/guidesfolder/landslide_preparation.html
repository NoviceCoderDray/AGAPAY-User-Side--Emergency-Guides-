Before a Landslide
Tips to Stay Prepared

This video shows how to prepare before a landslide.

Clip from: "LANDSLIDE-DISASTER PREPAREDNESS-BEFORE,DURING AND AFTER" by Daniel GARDETV on YouTube

1. Know if your area is prone to landslides

Learn if your home or community is in a landslide-prone zone.

Alamin kung ang inyong lugar ay kabilang sa mga lugar na madaling gumuho.

2. Prepare an emergency kit

Include food, water, flashlight, first aid, and important documents.

Maghanda ng emergency kit na may pagkain, tubig, flashlight, first aid, at mahahalagang dokumento.

3. Inspect and strengthen your home

Fix cracks, drainage, and avoid building near steep slopes.

Ayusin ang mga bitak at daluyan ng tubig, at iwasan ang pagtatayo malapit sa matarik na bahagi.

4. Make a family evacuation plan

Plan where to go and how to communicate during an emergency.

Gumawa ng plano ng pamilya kung saan pupunta at paano mag-uugnayan sa oras ng sakuna.

5. Monitor weather and news reports

Stay updated on heavy rains, earthquakes, and official warnings.

Manatiling updated sa malakas na ulan, lindol, at mga opisyal na babala.

6. Help reduce risks in your community

Plant trees and avoid cutting them down to stabilize the soil.

Magpunla at huwag magputol ng puno upang makatulong sa pagpapatibay ng lupa.
