CPR Basics
Life-Saving Steps

This video shows how to perform CPR on adults during emergencies.

A video from: "How to Conduct Hands only Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation or CPR?" by LIFESAVER on YouTube

1. Check for responsiveness

Tap the person and shout to see if they respond. If there's no response, call for help immediately.

Tapikin at tawagin ang tao upang makita kung siya ay tutugon. Kung walang tugon, humingi agad ng tulong.

2. Call emergency services

Call your local emergency number before starting CPR, or ask someone nearby to call.

Tawagan ang lokal na numero ng pang-emergency bago simulan ang CPR, o hilingin sa ibang tao na tumawag.

3. Start chest compressions

Push hard and fast in the center of the chest at a rate of 100–120 compressions per minute, about 2 inches deep.

Itulak nang malakas at mabilis sa gitna ng dibdib, 100–120 compressions kada minuto, mga 2 pulgada ang lalim.

4. Give rescue breaths (if trained)

Tilt the head, lift the chin, and give 2 breaths after every 30 compressions. Skip if untrained.

Itagilid ang ulo, itaas ang baba, at magbigay ng 2 paghinga pagkatapos ng bawat 30 compressions. Laktawan kung hindi sanay.

5. Continue until help arrives

Keep giving CPR until emergency responders take over or the person begins to breathe normally.

Ipagpatuloy ang pagbibigay ng CPR hanggang dumating ang mga tagapagligtas o kapag nagsimulang huminga nang normal ang pasyente.
