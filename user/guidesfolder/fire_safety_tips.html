Safety Tips
During a Fire

This is an offline fire safety video. Please watch carefully.

A video from: "Burn Prevention: What to do if there is a fire" by Children's National Hospital on YouTube

1. Stay calm and think clearly

Keep calm and don’t let fear take over. Focus on what to do next so you can act fast and safely.

Manatiling kalmado at huwag hayaang manaig ang takot. Ituon ang isip sa susunod na hakbang upang makakilos nang mabilis at ligtas.

2. Leave right away and never go back

Once you’re out of the house or building, stay out. Don’t go back for anything — your life is more important.

Kapag nakaalis ka na sa bahay o gusali, huwag nang bumalik. Huwag nang kunin pa ang anumang gamit — mas mahalaga ang iyong buhay.

3. Use the stairs — not the elevator

During a fire, elevators can stop working or trap you. Take the stairs to stay safe.

Huwag gumamit ng elevator sa oras ng sunog. Maaaring huminto ito o mabilanggo ka. Gumamit ng hagdan para sa kaligtasan.

4. Feel the door before you open

Use the back of your hand to check if the door is hot. If it is, don’t open it — the fire could be right outside.

Gamitin ang likod ng kamay upang suriin kung mainit ang pinto. Kung mainit ito, huwag buksan — maaaring nasa likod lang ang apoy.

5. Stay low and crawl under smoke

Smoke rises fast. If the place is filled with smoke, stay close to the floor where the air is clearer.

Ang usok ay mabilis tumaas. Kung mausok na ang paligid, gumapang nang mababa dahil mas malinaw ang hangin malapit sa sahig.

6. Stop, drop, and roll if on fire

If your clothes catch fire, don’t run. Stop, drop to the ground, and roll until the flames are out.

Kung nasunog ang iyong damit, huwag tumakbo. Huminto, humiga sa sahig, at gumulong hanggang mamatay ang apoy.

7. Ask for help as soon as you're safe

Once you're in a safe place, call 911 or ask someone to contact emergency services right away.

Kapag ligtas ka na, tumawag agad sa 911 o humingi ng tulong sa iba upang makontak ang mga awtoridad.

8. Go to your family’s safe meeting spot

Have a planned area outside where everyone will meet. It helps check if anyone is missing.

Magtakda ng lugar sa labas kung saan dapat magkita ang pamilya. Nakakatulong ito upang malaman kung may nawawala.
