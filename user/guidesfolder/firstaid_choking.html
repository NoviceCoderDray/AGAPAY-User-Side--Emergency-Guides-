Choking First Aid
How to Respond

This video demonstrates how to assist someone who is choking.

A video from: "How to help a Choking Person" by LIFESAVER on YouTube

1. Check if the person is choking

Ask “Are you choking?” If they can’t talk, cough, or breathe, they need help immediately.

Tanungin kung "Nabubulunan ka ba?" Kung hindi sila makapagsalita, umubo, o huminga, kailangan nila ng agarang tulong.

2. Encourage coughing (if mild)

If the person can cough, encourage them to continue. Coughing is the body’s way to clear the airway.

Kung kaya nilang umubo, hikayatin silang ipagpatuloy ito. Ang pag-ubo ay natural na paraan ng katawan upang alisin ang bara sa daanan ng hangin.

3. Perform abdominal thrusts (Heimlich)

Stand behind them, wrap your arms around their waist, and thrust inward and upward quickly.

Tumayo sa likod nila, yakapin ang kanilang baywang, at biglaang itulak papaloob at pataas.

4. Call emergency services

If the object doesn’t come out, call emergency services or have someone call while you continue helping.

Kung hindi mailabas ang bara, tumawag ng emergency services o ipatawag ito habang patuloy na tumutulong.

5. If person becomes unconscious

Lower them gently to the ground and begin CPR. Look for the object in their mouth and remove it if visible.

Ihiga sila ng dahan-dahan at simulan ang CPR. Tingnan kung may makikitang bara sa bibig at alisin ito kung posible.

6. Monitor breathing until help arrives

Keep checking their breathing and responsiveness while waiting for professionals to take over.

Patuloy na tingnan kung humihinga at tumutugon sila habang hinihintay ang mga propesyonal na dumating.
