Before a Typhoon
Preparation Tips

This video explains important preparations before a typhoon.

Clip from: "SCIENCE 8 Q2. Module 6 - What To Do BEFORE, DURING and AFTER Typhoon? Part 2" by Khristine Min Jin on YouTube

1. Secure your house

Check and repair your roof, windows, and walls to withstand strong winds.

Siguraduhin na matibay ang bubong, bintana, at pader ng bahay laban sa malakas na hangin.

2. Prepare an emergency kit

Stock up on food, water, flashlight, batteries, medicines, and other essentials.

Maghanda ng emergency kit na may pagkain, tubig, flashlight, baterya, gamot, at iba pang kailangan.

3. Know evacuation routes

Identify the nearest evacuation centers and safe routes before the storm hits.

Alamin ang pinakamalapit na evacuation center at ligtas na ruta bago dumating ang bagyo.

4. Store and protect important documents

Keep valuables, important documents, and supplies in waterproof containers.

Ilagay ang mahahalagang gamit at dokumento sa mga lalagyang hindi napapasukan ng tubig.

5. Monitor weather updates

Stay updated with news and warnings from PAGASA and local authorities.

Makinig sa balita at babala mula sa PAGASA at lokal na pamahalaan.

6. Help your neighbors

Assist the elderly, children, and people with disabilities in preparing for the typhoon.

Tumulong sa matatanda, bata, at PWD sa paghahanda bago ang bagyo.
