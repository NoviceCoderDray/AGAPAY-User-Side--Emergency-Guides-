After an Earthquake
Things You Should Do

This video shows what to do after an earthquake.

A clip from: "What To Do Before, During and After an Earthquake (Science 6)" by Teacher Apole on YouTube

1. Check yourself and others for injuries.

Administer first aid and seek medical help if needed.

Suriin ang sarili at ang iba kung may sugat. Magbigay ng paunang lunas at humingi ng tulong medikal kung kinakailangan.

2. Go to the nearest emergency exit.

Look at the surroundings and be careful as you look for a safe place.

Tumingin sa paligid at mag-ingat habang naghahanap ng ligtas na lugar.

3. Inspect your home for damage

Look for gas leaks, water damage, and structural hazards.

Suriin ang bahay kung may gas leak, sirang tubo, o pinsala sa estruktura.

4. Be prepared for aftershocks

Aftershocks may follow the main earthquake and can still cause damage.

Maghanda sa mga aftershock na maaaring sumunod at magdulot pa rin ng pinsala.

5. Listen to news and emergency updates

Stay informed about relief operations, safety instructions, or evacuations.

Makinig sa balita at mga anunsyo tungkol sa kaligtasan at relief operations.

6. Use your phone only for emergencies

Limit calls to avoid network congestion and preserve battery.

Gamitin lamang ang telepono para sa mahahalagang tawag upang maiwasan ang network congestion.

7. Help your neighbors, especially the elderly or injured

Work together with your community for recovery.

Tumulong sa mga kapitbahay lalo na ang matatanda o nasugatan.
