Before a Volcanic Eruption
Preparation Tips

This video explains important preparations before a volcanic eruption.

Clip from: "What to do before, during and after volcanic eruptions" by Yarah Jallorina on YouTube

1. Secure your house

Reinforce your roof and windows, and clear gutters to avoid ash buildup.

Siguraduhin na matibay ang bubong at bintana, at linisin ang alulod laban sa abo.

2. Prepare the emergency supplies

Include food, water, flashlight, batteries, and medicines.

Maghanda ng emergency kit na may pagkain, tubig, mask, goggles, flashlight, baterya, at gamot.

3. Prepare masks and goggles

Use N95 masks and goggles to protect your lungs and eyes from volcanic ash.

Maghanda ng N95 mask at goggles upang maprotektahan ang baga at mata laban sa abo.

4. Store clean water

Keep enough drinking water in sealed containers since ash may contaminate supplies.

Mag-imbak ng malinis na inuming tubig dahil maaaring maapektuhan ang suplay ng tubig ng abo.

5. Know evacuation routes

Identify evacuation centers and safe routes in case of eruption.

Alamin ang mga evacuation center at ligtas na ruta sakaling pumutok ang bulkan.

6. Stay informed

Listen to PHIVOLCS and local authorities for updates and warnings.

Makinig sa mga babala at update mula sa PHIVOLCS at lokal na pamahalaan.

7. Secure animals and livestock

Move pets and farm animals to safe areas away from ash and lava flow zones.

Ilipat ang mga alagang hayop at mga hayop sa bukid sa ligtas na lugar na malayo sa abo at lava.
