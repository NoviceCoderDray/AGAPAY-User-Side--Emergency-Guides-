After a Volcanic Eruption
Safety Tips

This video explains what to do after a volcanic eruption.

Clip from: "What to do before, during and after volcanic eruptions" by Yarah Jallorina on YouTube

1. Avoid ash

Stay away from ash-covered areas and wear a mask if you must go outside.

Iwasan ang mga lugar na may abo at magsuot ng mask kung kinakailangang lumabas.

2. Clean up safely

Remove ash from roofs carefully to prevent collapse. Wear protective gear.

Linisin ang bubong mula sa abo upang maiwasan ang pagbagsak. Magsuot ng proteksyon.

3. Check water supply

Do not drink contaminated water until declared safe by authorities.

Iwasan ang pag-inom ng maruming tubig hangga’t hindi sinasabing ligtas ng mga awtoridad.

4. Seek medical help

Visit a health center if you experience breathing problems or injuries.

Pumunta sa health center kung nakararanas ng hirap sa paghinga o may sugat.

5. Follow official updates

Listen to PHIVOLCS and local authorities for recovery information.

Makinig sa PHIVOLCS at lokal na pamahalaan para sa mga anunsyo ng pagbawi.

6. Return home only when safe

Do not go back until authorities confirm your area is safe.

Huwag bumalik hangga’t hindi sinasabing ligtas ng mga awtoridad ang inyong lugar.

7. Stay safe in evacuation centers

Keep your space clean, follow health protocols, and wait for further instructions.

Panatilihing malinis ang paligid, sundin ang health protocols, at hintayin ang susunod na abiso.
