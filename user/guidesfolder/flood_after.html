After a Flood
Safety Tips

This video explains important safety measures after a flood.

Clip from: "BEFORE, DURING AND AFTER A FLOOD" by Anna Joy Alcantara on YouTube

1. Return home safely

Only go back when authorities declare it safe. Avoid floodwaters on the way.

Bumalik lamang sa bahay kapag ligtas na ayon sa mga awtoridad. Iwasan ang baha sa daan.

2. Inspect your house

Check for damage, cracks, or electrical hazards before entering.

Suriin ang bahay para sa pinsala, bitak, o panganib sa kuryente bago pumasok.

3. Avoid floodwater

Do not touch floodwater as it may be contaminated with sewage and chemicals.

Iwasang hawakan ang baha dahil maaaring may dumi at kemikal ito.

4. Clean and disinfect

Wash and disinfect furniture, utensils, and surfaces that touched floodwater.

Linisin at disimpektahin ang mga gamit at lugar na nadikitan ng baha.

5. Use safe drinking water

Boil or treat water before drinking to prevent waterborne diseases.

Pakuluan o gamitan ng purifier ang tubig bago inumin upang maiwasan ang sakit.

6. Seek medical help if needed

If you feel sick or injured after the flood, seek medical attention immediately.

Kung may nararamdamang sakit o pinsala, agad na magpatingin sa doktor.

7. Stay updated

Follow local government announcements for relief assistance and safety reminders.

Sundin ang mga abiso ng lokal na pamahalaan tungkol sa relief at mga paalala sa kaligtasan.
