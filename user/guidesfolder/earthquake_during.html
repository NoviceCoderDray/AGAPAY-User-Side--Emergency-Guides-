During an Earthquake
Tips to Stay Safe

This video demonstrates what to do during an earthquake.

A clip from: "What To Do Before, During and After an Earthquake (Science 6)" by Teacher Apole on YouTube

1. Stay calm and don't panic

Keep calm and act quickly. Staying calm helps you think clearly and respond safely. Panicking can lead to poor decisions or injuries during the earthquake.

Manatiling kalmado at huwag mag-panic. Ang pagiging kalmado ay makakatulong sa tamang pag-iisip at ligtas na kilos habang lumilindol.

2. Drop, Cover, and Hold On

Immediately get down on your hands and knees, cover your head and neck, and hold on to something sturdy.

Agad na yumuko, magtakip ng ulo, at kumapit sa matibay na bagay.

3. Stay away from windows and glass

Move to a safer area where nothing can fall or shatter near you.

Lumayo sa mga bintana o salamin upang maiwasan ang aksidente mula sa mga nabasag na bagay.

4. Do not use elevators

Use stairs instead. Elevators may malfunction or trap you inside during an earthquake.

Gamitin ang hagdan sa halip na elevator. Maaaring masira o maipit sa elevator habang lumilindol.

5. Stay indoors until the shaking stops

It’s safer to remain inside and avoid falling debris from outside.

Mas ligtas na manatili sa loob ng gusali hangga’t hindi pa natatapos ang lindol.

6. If outside, move to an open space

Stay away from buildings, trees, power lines, and streetlights.

Kung nasa labas, lumipat sa bukas na espasyo na malayo sa mga gusali, puno, at kable ng kuryente.

7. If in a car, stop in a safe place

Pull over safely, avoid stopping under bridges or near buildings, and stay inside the vehicle.

Kung nasa loob ng sasakyan, huminto sa ligtas na lugar at huwag dumaan sa ilalim ng tulay o malapit sa gusali.
