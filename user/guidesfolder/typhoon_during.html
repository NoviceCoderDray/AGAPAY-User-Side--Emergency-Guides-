During a Typhoon
Tips to Stay Safe

This video shows safety measures to follow during a typhoon.

Clip from: "SCIENCE 8 Q2. Module 6 - What To Do BEFORE, DURING and AFTER Typhoon? Part 2" by Khristine Min Jin on YouTube

1. Stay indoors

Remain inside a sturdy house and avoid going outside until authorities declare it safe.

Manatili sa loob ng matibay na bahay at huwag lalabas hangga’t hindi sinasabi ng awtoridad na ligtas na.

2. Keep away from windows

Strong winds can break glass—stay in safer parts of the house.

Lumayo sa mga bintana dahil maaaring mabasag ng malakas na hangin.

3. Be alert for flooding

Move to higher ground if your area is prone to floods.

Agad na pumunta sa mataas na lugar kung ang inyong lugar ay madalas bahain.

4. Turn off utilities if necessary

Switch off electricity and gas to prevent accidents.

Patayin ang kuryente at gas upang maiwasan ang aksidente.

5. Follow official instructions

Stay tuned to the radio, TV, or official social media for updates.

Makinig sa radyo, TV, o opisyal na social media para sa mga abiso at babala.

6. Use your emergency supply

Have your supplies ready in case the typhoon lasts for hours or days.

Gamitin ang mga nakahandang gamit kung tatagal ang bagyo ng ilang oras o araw.

7. Be ready to evacuate

Prepare to leave your home immediately if advised by authorities. Bring only essential items.

Maging handa na lumikas agad kung kinakailangan ayon sa utos ng awtoridad. Dalhin lamang ang mga mahahalagang gamit.
